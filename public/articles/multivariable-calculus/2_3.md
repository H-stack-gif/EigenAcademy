# Multivariable Calculus Topic 2.3: Arc Length and Curvature

How long is a curve? This seemingly simple question leads to beautiful mathematics. For a straight line, length is just distance. But curves wind and twist—measuring their length requires calculus. We approximate the curve with tiny line segments, sum their lengths, and take a limit. The resulting **arc length formula** integrates the speed along the curve. Every GPS device computing "distance to destination" along roads uses this principle.

Beyond length, curves have **curvature**—a measure of how sharply they bend. A straight line has zero curvature. A small circle curves sharply; a large circle curves gently. Curvature quantifies this precisely, determining the "tightness" of a turn. This matters in railroad design (trains derail on sharp curves), highway engineering (speed limits depend on curvature), and computer graphics (smooth curves require controlled curvature).

This article develops arc length and curvature rigorously. We'll derive the arc length formula, compute lengths of specific curves, parametrize curves by arc length (the most natural parametrization), and define curvature multiple ways. We'll introduce the **TNB frame**—tangent, normal, and binormal vectors that move with a particle, providing a coordinate system adapted to the curve. This geometric machinery describes how curves sit in space, essential for differential geometry and understanding motion along arbitrary paths.

## Arc Length

**Arc length** from $t = a$ to $t = b$:
$$s = \int_a^b |\mathbf{r}'(t)| \, dt = \int_a^b \sqrt{[x'(t)]^2 + [y'(t)]^2 + [z'(t)]^2} \, dt$$

**Interpretation**: Total distance traveled along curve

**Speed**: $v(t) = |\mathbf{r}'(t)|$ (scalar)

**Arc length** is the integral of speed.

### Example 1: Length of Helix Segment

Find the length of the helix $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle \cos t, \sin t, t \rangle$ from $t = 0$ to $t = 2\pi$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle -\sin t, \cos t, 1 \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{\sin^2 t + \cos^2 t + 1} = \sqrt{2}$$

$$s = \int_0^{2\pi} \sqrt{2} \, dt = \sqrt{2} \cdot 2\pi = 2\pi\sqrt{2}$$

**Answer:** Arc length $= 2\pi\sqrt{2} \approx 8.89$ units

### Example 2: Length of Circular Arc

Find the length of $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle 3\cos t, 3\sin t \rangle$ from $t = 0$ to $t = \pi/2$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle -3\sin t, 3\cos t \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{9\sin^2 t + 9\cos^2 t} = \sqrt{9} = 3$$

$$s = \int_0^{\pi/2} 3 \, dt = 3 \cdot \frac{\pi}{2} = \frac{3\pi}{2}$$

**Verification**: Quarter circle of radius 3 has arc length $ \frac{1}{4}(2\pi \cdot 3) = \frac{3\pi}{2}$ ✓

**Answer:** Arc length $=  \frac{3\pi}{2}$

### Example 3: Length Requiring Integration

Find the length of $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle t, t^2, t^3 \rangle$ from $t = 0$ to $t = 1$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle 1, 2t, 3t^2 \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{1 + 4t^2 + 9t^4}$$

$$s = \int_0^1 \sqrt{1 + 4t^2 + 9t^4} \, dt$$

This integral doesn't have elementary antiderivative.

**Numerical approximation**: $s \approx 1.44$

**Answer:** $s =  \int_0^1 \sqrt{1 + 4t^2 + 9t^4} \, dt \approx 1.44$

## Arc Length Function

**Arc length function** (measured from $t = a$):
$$s(t) = \int_a^t |\mathbf{r}'(u)| \, du$$

**Properties**:
- $s(a) = 0$
- $ \frac{ds}{dt} = |\mathbf{r}'(t)| = $ speed

### Example 4: Arc Length Function for Helix

Find $s(t)$ for helix $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle 2\cos t, 2\sin t, 3t \rangle$ starting at $t = 0$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle -2\sin t, 2\cos t, 3 \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{4\sin^2 t + 4\cos^2 t + 9} = \sqrt{4 + 9} = \sqrt{13}$$

$$s(t) = \int_0^t \sqrt{13} \, du = \sqrt{13} \cdot t$$

**Answer:** $s(t) = \sqrt{13} \, t$

## Parametrization by Arc Length

**Arc length parametrization**: Use $s$ instead of $t$ as parameter.

From $s(t) =  \int_a^t |\mathbf{r}'(u)| \, du$, solve for $t = t(s)$, then $\mathbf{r}(s) = \mathbf{r}(t(s))$.

**Property**: $ \left|\frac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds}\right| = 1$ (unit speed parametrization)

### Example 5: Reparametrizing Helix

Reparametrize $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle \cos t, \sin t, t \rangle$ by arc length.

**Solution:**

From Example 1: $|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{2}$

$$s = \int_0^t \sqrt{2} \, du = \sqrt{2} \, t$$

Solve for $t$: $t =  \frac{s}{\sqrt{2}}$

Substitute:
$$\mathbf{r}(s) = \left\langle \cos\frac{s}{\sqrt{2}}, \sin\frac{s}{\sqrt{2}}, \frac{s}{\sqrt{2}} \right\rangle$$

**Verify**: $ \frac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds} = \left\langle -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\sin\frac{s}{\sqrt{2}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\cos\frac{s}{\sqrt{2}}, \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \right\rangle$

$$\left|\frac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds}\right| = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2}\sin^2 + \frac{1}{2}\cos^2 + \frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2}} = 1$$ ✓

**Answer:** $\mathbf{r}(s) = \left\langle \cos \frac{s}{\sqrt{2}}, \sin\frac{s}{\sqrt{2}}, \frac{s}{\sqrt{2}} \right\rangle$

## Curvature: Definition

**Curvature** measures rate of change of unit tangent vector:

**Definition**: $ \kappa = \left|\frac{d\mathbf{T}}{ds}\right|$

where $\mathbf{T}$ is unit tangent vector and $s$ is arc length.

**Alternative formula** (in terms of $t$):
$$\kappa = \frac{|\mathbf{T}'(t)|}{|\mathbf{r}'(t)|} = \frac{|\mathbf{r}'(t) \times \mathbf{r}''(t)|}{|\mathbf{r}'(t)|^3}$$

**Radius of curvature**: $ \rho = \frac{1}{\kappa}$

### Example 6: Curvature of Circle

Find curvature of circle $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle a\cos t, a\sin t \rangle$ of radius $a$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle -a\sin t, a\cos t \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = a$$

$$\mathbf{T}(t) = \frac{1}{a}\langle -a\sin t, a\cos t \rangle = \langle -\sin t, \cos t \rangle$$

$$\mathbf{T}'(t) = \langle -\cos t, -\sin t \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{T}'(t)| = 1$$

$$\kappa = \frac{1}{a}$$

**Answer:** Curvature $=  \frac{1}{a}$ (constant, as expected for circle)

### Example 7: Curvature of Line

Find curvature of line $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle t, 2t, 3t \rangle$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle 1, 2, 3 \rangle$$ (constant)

$$\mathbf{T}(t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{14}}\langle 1, 2, 3 \rangle$$ (constant)

$$\mathbf{T}'(t) = \mathbf{0}$$

$$\kappa = 0$$

**Answer:** Curvature $= 0$ (straight lines don't curve)

### Example 8: Using Cross Product Formula

Find curvature of $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle t, t^2, 0 \rangle$ (parabola in plane).

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle 1, 2t, 0 \rangle$$

$$\mathbf{r}''(t) = \langle 0, 2, 0 \rangle$$

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) \times \mathbf{r}''(t) = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 1 & 2t & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = \langle 0, 0, 2 \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t) \times \mathbf{r}''(t)| = 2$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{1 + 4t^2}$$

$$\kappa = \frac{2}{(1 + 4t^2)^{3/2}}$$

At $t = 0$ (vertex): $\kappa = 2$

As $t \to \pm\infty$: $\kappa \to 0$ (curve straightens out)

**Answer:** $\kappa(t) =  \frac{2}{(1+4t^2)^{3/2}}$

## Principal Unit Normal Vector

**Unit tangent**: $ \mathbf{T} = \frac{\mathbf{r}'}{|\mathbf{r}'|}$

**Principal unit normal**: $ \mathbf{N} = \frac{\mathbf{T}'}{|\mathbf{T}'|}$

**Properties**:
- $\mathbf{N} \perp \mathbf{T}$ (perpendicular to tangent)
- Points toward "inside" of curve
- $\kappa =  \frac{|\mathbf{T}'|}{|\mathbf{r}'|}$, so $\mathbf{T}' = \kappa|\mathbf{r}'|\mathbf{N}$

### Example 9: Finding Normal Vector

Find $\mathbf{N}(0)$ for $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle \cos t, \sin t, t \rangle$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle -\sin t, \cos t, 1 \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{T}(t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\langle -\sin t, \cos t, 1 \rangle$$

$$\mathbf{T}'(t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\langle -\cos t, -\sin t, 0 \rangle$$

$$\mathbf{T}'(0) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\langle -1, 0, 0 \rangle$$

$$|\mathbf{T}'(0)| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$

$$\mathbf{N}(0) = \frac{\mathbf{T}'(0)}{|\mathbf{T}'(0)|} = \frac{\langle -1, 0, 0 \rangle / \sqrt{2}}{1/\sqrt{2}} = \langle -1, 0, 0 \rangle = -\mathbf{i}$$

**Answer:** $\mathbf{N}(0) = -\mathbf{i}$ (points toward $z$-axis, center of circular projection)

## Binormal Vector and TNB Frame

**Binormal vector**: $\mathbf{B} = \mathbf{T} \times \mathbf{N}$

**TNB frame**: Orthonormal basis $\{\mathbf{T}, \mathbf{N}, \mathbf{B}\}$ moving with particle

**Properties**:
- $\mathbf{T} \cdot \mathbf{N} = 0$, $\mathbf{N} \cdot \mathbf{B} = 0$, $\mathbf{B} \cdot \mathbf{T} = 0$
- $|\mathbf{T}| = |\mathbf{N}| = |\mathbf{B}| = 1$
- Forms right-handed coordinate system

### Example 10: Complete TNB Frame

Find $\mathbf{T}$, $\mathbf{N}$, $\mathbf{B}$ at $t = 0$ for $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle t, t^2, t^3 \rangle$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle 1, 2t, 3t^2 \rangle$, $|\mathbf{r}'(0)| = 1$

$\mathbf{T}(t) =  \frac{\langle 1, 2t, 3t^2 \rangle}{\sqrt{1+4t^2+9t^4}}$

At $t=0$: $\mathbf{T}(0) = \langle 1, 0, 0 \rangle$

Computing $\mathbf{T}'(0)$ using quotient rule: $\mathbf{T}'(0) = \langle 0, 2, 0 \rangle$

$\mathbf{N}(0) =  \frac{\langle 0, 2, 0 \rangle}{2} = \langle 0, 1, 0 \rangle$

$$\mathbf{B}(0) = \mathbf{T}(0) \times \mathbf{N}(0) = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 & 0 \end{vmatrix} = \langle 0, 0, 1 \rangle$$

**Answer:** $\mathbf{T}(0) = \mathbf{i}$, $\mathbf{N}(0) = \mathbf{j}$, $\mathbf{B}(0) = \mathbf{k}$

## Formulas Summary

**For plane curves** $y = f(x)$:
$$\kappa = \frac{|f''(x)|}{[1+(f'(x))^2]^{3/2}}$$

**Components of acceleration**:
$$\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{T}} + \mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{N}}$$

where:
- $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{T}} = v' \mathbf{T}$ (tangential component)
- $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{N}} = \kappa v^2 \mathbf{N}$ (normal component)

### Example 11: Curvature of $y = x^2$

Find curvature of parabola $y = x^2$ at origin.

**Solution:**

$f(x) = x^2$, $f'(x) = 2x$, $f''(x) = 2$

At $x = 0$:
$$\kappa = \frac{|2|}{[1+0]^{3/2}} = 2$$

**Answer:** Curvature at origin $= 2$ (matches Example 8!)

### Example 12: Components of Acceleration

For circular motion $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle a\cos\omega t, a\sin\omega t \rangle$, find $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{T}}$ and $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{N}}$.

**Solution:**

$$\mathbf{v}(t) = \langle -a\omega\sin\omega t, a\omega\cos\omega t \rangle$$

$$v = |\mathbf{v}| = a\omega$$ (constant)

$$v' = 0$$, so $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{T}} = 0$

From Example 6: $\kappa = 1/a$

$$\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{N}} = \kappa v^2 = \frac{1}{a}(a\omega)^2 = a\omega^2$$

**Verify**: $\mathbf{a}(t) = \langle -a\omega^2\cos\omega t, -a\omega^2\sin\omega t \rangle$

$|\mathbf{a}| = a\omega^2$ ✓, points toward center (normal direction) ✓

**Answer:** $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{T}} = 0$, $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{N}} = a\omega^2$

### Example 13: Torsion (Preview)

**Torsion** $\tau$ measures how much curve twists out of plane:
$$\tau = -\mathbf{N} \cdot \mathbf{B}'$$

For helix $\mathbf{r}(t) = \langle a\cos t, a\sin t, bt \rangle$:
- Curvature: $\kappa =  \frac{a}{a^2+b^2}$
- Torsion: $\tau =  \frac{b}{a^2+b^2}$

**Answer:** Circular helix has constant curvature and torsion.

## Answer Key

**Problem 1: C**
$\mathbf{r}'(t) = \langle 3, 4 \rangle$
$|\mathbf{r}'(t)| = \sqrt{9+16} = 5$
$s = \int_0^1 5 \, dt = 5$
This is 3-4-5 right triangle scaled by $t$

**Problem 2: B**
Arc length parametrization means unit speed
By definition: $ \left|\frac{d\mathbf{r}}{ds}\right| = 1$
This is why arc length is "natural" parameter
Makes formulas simpler (e.g., $\kappa = |\mathbf{T}'|$ directly)

**Problem 3: B**
Curvature of circle = reciprocal of radius
Small circle (small $r$) curves sharply (large $\kappa$)
Large circle (large $r$) curves gently (small $\kappa$)
$\kappa = 1/r$
Verified in Example 6

**Problem 4: B**
$\mathbf{N} = \mathbf{T}'/|\mathbf{T}'|$ perpendicular to $\mathbf{T}$
Since $|\mathbf{T}| = 1$ constant, $\mathbf{T} \cdot \mathbf{T}' = 0$
$\mathbf{N}$ points toward "inside" of curve
Part of orthonormal TNB frame

**Problem 5: C**
$\mathbf{a} = v'\mathbf{T} + \kappa v^2\mathbf{N}$
If $v$ constant: $v' = 0$
Tangential component: $\mathbf{a}_{\mathbf{T}} = 0$
All acceleration is normal (centripetal)
Example: uniform circular motion
