# 4.1: Derivation and Physical Interpretation

The heat equation models diffusion of heat (or other quantities) in a medium. This article covers:

- Derivation from conservation of energy and Fourier's law
- Physical interpretation and the diffusion coefficient (thermal diffusivity)
- Simple examples and dimensional analysis

## Practice problem

**Answer:** True
