# 7.4: Applications to Wave and Diffusion Equations

Transform method applications: using Fourier and Laplace transforms to treat wave and diffusion equations; inversion and interpretation of solutions.

Topics covered:
- Transform pairs and inverse transforms used in PDEs
- Examples: heat equation on R, wave equation Fourier solutions
- Convolution representations and physical interpretation

## Practice problem

1) Which transform is most naturally used for time-initial-value problems with exponential decay? (Answer: Laplace transform)
