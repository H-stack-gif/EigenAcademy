# 8.6: Method of Images

Method of images for building Green's functions that satisfy simple boundary conditions: mirrored sources, constructing solutions on half-spaces, and examples for electrostatics and diffusion.

Topics covered:
- The image source method and example geometries
- How mirrored sources enforce homogeneous boundary conditions
- Limitations and typical examples
