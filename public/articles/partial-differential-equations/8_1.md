# 8.1: Sturm-Liouville Problems

Introduction to Sturm-Liouville eigenvalue problems: self-adjoint operators, boundary conditions, and how eigenvalues/eigenfunctions arise in PDE separations.

Topics covered:
- Definition of Sturm-Liouville form and examples
- Boundary conditions and orthogonality
- Role in separation of variables for PDEs

## Practice problem

1) True/False: Sturm-Liouville eigenfunctions corresponding to different eigenvalues are orthogonal under the weight function.

**Answer:** True
