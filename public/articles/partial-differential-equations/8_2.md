# 8.2: Orthogonality of Eigenfunctions

Orthogonality and completeness of eigenfunction sets from Sturm-Liouville problems — inner-products, weight functions, and completeness statements.

Topics covered:
- Inner product spaces for Sturm-Liouville problems
- Orthogonality relations and examples
- Completeness and expansion theorems

## Practice problem

1) True/False: Eigenfunctions of a regular Sturm-Liouville problem form a complete set for representing suitable functions.

**Answer:** True
