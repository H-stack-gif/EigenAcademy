# Organic Chemistry — Topic 1.1: Structure and Bonding

Short primer on covalent bonding, hybridization, resonance, and molecular structure. This topic establishes how organic molecules are built and how structure determines reactivity.

## Practice Problems

1. Draw the Lewis structure and predict hybridization for methane, ethene, and acetylene.
2. Explain why resonance stabilizes certain organic ions and give two examples.

## Answer Key

1. Methane: sp3, ethene: sp2, acetylene: sp. Show electron-pair diagrams.
2. Resonance delocalizes charge — examples: acetate ion, benzyl cation/anion.
