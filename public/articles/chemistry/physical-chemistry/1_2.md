# Physical Chemistry Topic 1.2: Reaction Kinetics

Introduction to reaction rates, rate laws, reaction order, and the Arrhenius equation. We'll include experiments that determine rate laws and practice with integrated rate laws for zero-, first-, and second-order reactions.

## Practice Problems

1. For a reaction A -> products that follows first-order kinetics with rate constant k, derive the integrated form and show how you would determine k from experimental [A] vs. time data.
2. Explain qualitatively how temperature affects reaction rates using the Arrhenius equation.

## Answer Key

1. Integrated 1st order: [A](t) = [A]0 e^{-kt}. Plot ln[A] vs time to find slope = -k.
2. Arrhenius: k = A e^{-Ea/(RT)} — increasing T decreases exponent magnitude and so increases k; explain pre-exponential factor role.
