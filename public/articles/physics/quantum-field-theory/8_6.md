# Quantum Field Theory Topic 8.6: Quantum Gravity and String Theory (Brief)

The unification of quantum mechanics and general relativity remains the central unsolved problem in theoretical physics. General relativity describes gravity as spacetime curvature governed by the Einstein-Hilbert action $S = \int d^4x\sqrt{-g}M_{\text{Pl}}^2 R/2$ where $R$ is the Ricci scalar and $M_{\text{Pl}} = 1.22 \times 10^{19}$ GeV is the Planck mass, while quantum field theory treats particles as excitations of quantum fields on a fixed spacetime background. Attempting to quantize gravity perturbatively leads to non-renormalizable divergences: the graviton propagator $\sim 1/p^2$ combined with derivative couplings $\sim p^2/M_{\text{Pl}}^2$ from the Einstein-Hilbert action makes loop integrals diverge as $\int d^4p\, p^2 \sim \Lambda^4/M_{\text{Pl}}^2$, worse at each loop order. As an effective field theory, general relativity is valid below the Planck scale with higher-derivative corrections $\sim R^2/M_{\text{Pl}}^2$ suppressed at low energies, but a UV completion is required. String theory proposes replacing point particles with one-dimensional extended objects (strings) with characteristic length $\ell_s \sim 10^{-35}$ m, smearing out interactions and rendering the theory finite. Consistency requires supersymmetry and extra dimensions: superstring theories exist in 10 spacetime dimensions, while M-theory (the 11-dimensional framework unifying the five consistent string theories) provides the current frontier of unification attempts.

The effective field theory approach to quantum gravity recognizes that GR is an excellent description at accessible energies despite being non-renormalizable. The Lagrangian:

$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{EFT}} = \frac{M_{\text{Pl}}^2}{2}R + c_1 R^2 + c_2 R_{\mu\nu}R^{\mu\nu} + \cdots$$

contains the Einstein-Hilbert term plus higher-derivative operators suppressed by powers of $M_{\text{Pl}}$. At energies $E \ll M_{\text{Pl}}$, the corrections are tiny: $c_1 R^2/M_{\text{Pl}}^2 \sim (E/M_{\text{Pl}})^2 R$. This explains why quantum corrections to gravity are unobservably small in current experiments. The breakdown occurs near the Planck scale where quantum fluctuations of spacetime geometry become order-one, requiring a UV-complete theory.

String theory offers the leading candidate for UV completion. In string theory, the fundamental objects are strings—one-dimensional entities that vibrate. Different vibrational modes correspond to different particles: the zero-mode gives massless particles (photon, graviton), while excited modes have masses $m_n^2 = n/\alpha'$ where $\alpha' = \ell_s^2$ is the string tension. The crucial feature: closed strings automatically include a massless spin-2 mode—the graviton. This emergence of gravity from strings, rather than being imposed, is string theory's most compelling feature.

### Example 1: String Length and Planck Scale

Relate the string length $\ell_s$ to the Planck length and show why quantum gravity effects are suppressed at low energies.

**Solution:**

The Planck length:

$$\ell_{\text{Pl}} = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar G_N}{c^3}} = \frac{1}{M_{\text{Pl}}} \approx \frac{1}{1.22 \times 10^{19}\text{ GeV}} \approx 1.6 \times 10^{-35}\text{ m}$$

The string length $\ell_s$ is the characteristic size of a string. The relation to Planck length depends on string coupling $g_s$:

$$\ell_{\text{Pl}}^2 \sim g_s^2 \ell_s^2$$

For perturbative string theory $g_s \ll 1$, so $\ell_s \sim \ell_{\text{Pl}}/g_s$ can be larger than Planck length.

**Quantum gravity effects**: String corrections to scattering amplitudes come in powers of $\alpha' \sim \ell_s^2$:

$$\mathcal{A} = \mathcal{A}_{\text{SUGRA}} + \alpha' s \mathcal{A}^{(1)} + (\alpha' s)^2\mathcal{A}^{(2)} + \cdots$$

where $s$ is the Mandelstam variable (energy squared). For $E \ll 1/\ell_s$:

$$\alpha' s \sim \left(\frac{E\ell_s}{\hbar c}\right)^2 \sim \left(\frac{E}{M_{\text{string}}}\right)^2 \ll 1$$

The corrections are suppressed! At accessible energies $E \sim$ TeV, $E/M_{\text{Pl}} \sim 10^{-16}$, quantum gravity effects are negligible. Only near Planck scale do finite-size string effects become important, regulating UV divergences.

**Answer:** String length $\ell_s \sim 10^{-35}$ m; quantum gravity corrections $\sim (E\ell_s)^2$ suppressed at $E \ll M_{\text{string}} \sim M_{\text{Pl}}$, observable only near Planck scale.

The requirement of 10 dimensions comes from quantum consistency. The worldsheet of a propagating string sweeps out a two-dimensional surface parametrized by coordinates $(\tau, \sigma)$. Quantum fluctuations of this worldsheet must cancel anomalies (Weyl invariance, diffeomorphism invariance) for consistency. The critical dimension is:

- Bosonic string: $D = 26$ (has tachyon, no fermions, unstable)
- Superstring: $D = 10$ (includes fermions via worldsheet SUSY)

In fewer dimensions, the theory has anomalies and is inconsistent. In more dimensions, it also fails. The five consistent superstring theories—Type I, Type IIA, Type IIB, Heterotic SO(32), Heterotic $E_8 \times E_8$—all live in 10 dimensions and are related by dualities, suggesting they're different limits of a single 11-dimensional M-theory.

### Example 2: Graviton as String Mode

Show that closed string theory contains a massless spin-2 graviton mode.

**Solution:**

The closed string has left-moving and right-moving oscillators $\alpha_n^\mu$ and $\tilde{\alpha}_n^\mu$. The state:

$$|\text{graviton}\rangle = \epsilon_{\mu\nu}\alpha_{-1}^\mu\tilde{\alpha}_{-1}^\nu|0\rangle$$

is created by acting with lowest oscillators on the vacuum. The polarization tensor $\epsilon_{\mu\nu}$ is symmetric (closed string) and traceless (physical state condition). The mass:

$$m^2 = \frac{1}{\alpha'}(N_L + N_R - 2)$$

where $N_L = N_R = 1$ for this state (level-matching condition), giving:

$$m^2 = 0$$

It's massless! The helicity: left-mover and right-mover each carry spin $\pm 1$, combining to total spin $|1 + 1| = 2$ or $|1 - 1| = 0$. The spin-2 component is the graviton.

The coupling to matter is determined by worldsheet conformal invariance. The string couples to background metric $g_{\mu\nu}$ through:

$$S = \frac{1}{4\pi\alpha'}\int d^2\sigma\sqrt{-h}h^{ab}g_{\mu\nu}(X)\partial_a X^\mu\partial_b X^\nu$$

Expanding $g_{\mu\nu} = \eta_{\mu\nu} + h_{\mu\nu}/M_{\text{Pl}}$ and computing scattering amplitudes reproduces GR with Newton constant $G_N \sim g_s^2\ell_s^{d-2}$ in $d$ dimensions.

**Compactification**: The extra 6 dimensions must be compactified on a small manifold (Calabi-Yau for $\mathcal{N}=1$ SUSY in 4D). The 4D graviton is the zero-mode of 10D graviton reduced on the compact space.

**Answer:** Closed string ground state includes massless spin-2 mode (graviton); coupling to metric fixed by worldsheet consistency reproduces GR with $G_N \sim g_s^2\ell_s^8$.

Extra dimensions provide a rich phenomenology. Kaluza-Klein compactification of a dimension of radius $R$ gives a tower of massive modes with masses $m_n = n/R$. For large extra dimensions (ADD scenario), the fundamental Planck scale could be as low as $M_* \sim$ TeV if the extra dimensions are large ($R \sim 10^{-3}$ m for 2 dimensions), solving the hierarchy problem by diluting gravity in the bulk. Warped extra dimensions (Randall-Sundrum) use non-factorizable metric to generate exponential hierarchies naturally.

Observational tests of quantum gravity are challenging but improving. Gravitational waves from LIGO/Virgo provide new windows: quantum corrections could affect the waveform from black hole mergers at Planck-scale distances. The cosmic microwave background might carry imprints of quantum gravity from inflation. Black hole thermodynamics (Bekenstein-Hawking entropy $S = A/(4G_N)$) suggests deep connections between gravity and quantum information, with holography (AdS/CFT correspondence) revealing that quantum gravity in $(d+1)$ dimensions is equivalent to a CFT in $d$ dimensions without gravity—a revolutionary perspective linking quantum gravity to gauge theory.

### Example 3: Hawking Radiation and Black Hole Thermodynamics

Derive the Hawking temperature for a Schwarzschild black hole.

**Solution:**

The Schwarzschild metric:

$$ds^2 = -\left(1 - \frac{2GM}{r}\right)dt^2 + \left(1 - \frac{2GM}{r}\right)^{-1}dr^2 + r^2d\Omega^2$$

Near the horizon $r = r_s = 2GM$, define $\rho = r - r_s$:

$$ds^2 \approx -\frac{\rho}{r_s}dt^2 + \frac{r_s}{\rho}d\rho^2 + r_s^2d\Omega^2$$

Change to Euclidean time $t_E = it$ and tortoise coordinate to make this look like Rindler space. The periodicity in Euclidean time required to avoid conical singularity at $\rho = 0$ is:

$$\beta = 4\pi r_s = 8\pi GM$$

This periodicity corresponds to temperature (thermal periodicity $\beta = 1/T$):

$$T_H = \frac{1}{8\pi GM} = \frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi G M k_B} \approx \frac{6.2 \times 10^{-8}\text{ K}}{M/M_\odot}$$

For solar-mass black hole: $T_H \sim 10^{-7}$ K (unobservably cold). For micro black holes $M \sim M_{\text{Pl}} \sim 10^{-5}$ g: $T_H \sim 10^{32}$ K!

**Entropy**: From thermodynamics $dS = dE/T$, integrating gives Bekenstein-Hawking entropy:

$$S_{BH} = \frac{k_B c^3 A}{4G\hbar} = \frac{A}{4\ell_{\text{Pl}}^2}$$

where $A = 4\pi r_s^2$ is horizon area. For solar-mass BH: $S \sim 10^{77} k_B$ (enormous!).

**Quantum gravity**: The entropy scales as area, not volume—suggesting holography (information on surface). String theory reproduces this precisely by counting microstates of D-branes for extremal BHs. The factor of 1/4 from QFT in curved space matches the microscopic counting—a triumph of string theory.

**Answer:** Hawking temperature $T_H = \hbar c^3/(8\pi GM k_B) \sim 10^{-7}(M_\odot/M)$ K; entropy $S = A/(4\ell_{\text{Pl}}^2)$ (area law) suggests holography.

Loop quantum gravity (LQG) takes an alternative approach, quantizing spacetime geometry directly without strings. The fundamental objects are spin networks—graphs with edges labeled by SU(2) representations carrying geometric information. The Hilbert space is built from these combinatorial structures, and area and volume become discrete operators with quantized eigenvalues. The Planck-scale discreteness of geometry emerges naturally, potentially resolving the big bang singularity through quantum bounce scenarios. However, LQG faces challenges connecting to Standard Model matter and demonstrating Lorentz invariance in the continuum limit.

The landscape problem in string theory arises from the vast number of possible compactifications. Estimates suggest $\sim 10^{500}$ distinct vacua (string landscape), each giving different 4D physics (particle content, coupling constants). Without a principle selecting the correct vacuum, string theory loses predictive power. Anthropic reasoning (we observe this vacuum because others don't allow observers) is philosophically unsettling but may be unavoidable. The swampland program attempts to identify consistency conditions that constrain effective field theories as genuine string compactifications, potentially narrowing possibilities.

Despite the challenges, quantum gravity research has produced profound insights: holographic principle relating degrees of freedom to area rather than volume, black hole information paradox driving developments in quantum information theory, emergence of spacetime itself from more fundamental quantum entanglement, and connections between gravity and thermodynamics suggesting gravitational dynamics might be thermodynamic in origin. Whether the final theory is string theory, loop quantum gravity, or something yet undiscovered, the quest to unify quantum mechanics and general relativity continues to drive fundamental physics forward, even as experimental tests remain frustratingly out of reach for the foreseeable future.

## Answer Key

**Problem 1: B** — GR Lagrangian $\sim M_{\text{Pl}}^2 R$ has dimension-2 coupling. Graviton propagator $\sim 1/p^2$, vertices $\sim p^2/M_{\text{Pl}}^2$. Loop integrals: $\int d^4p\, p^{2n}/M_{\text{Pl}}^{2n} \sim \Lambda^{2n+4}/M_{\text{Pl}}^{2n}$ diverge increasingly badly. One-loop $\sim \Lambda^4/M_{\text{Pl}}^2$, two-loop worse. Non-renormalizable—infinite counterterms needed. **EFT approach**: valid below $M_{\text{Pl}}$, higher derivatives suppressed; requires UV completion (strings, LQG).

**Problem 2: B** — Worldsheet quantum consistency (Weyl anomaly cancellation) requires critical dimension: bosonic string $D=26$, superstring $D=10$. Five consistent superstrings (Type I, IIA, IIB, Heterotic $E_8 \times E_8$, SO(32)) all in 10D, related by dualities (S, T, U). M-theory unifies them in 11D. Extra 6-7 dimensions compactified on Calabi-Yau (preserving $\mathcal{N}=1$ SUSY in 4D). Large or warped extra dimensions modify phenomenology.

**Problem 3: B** — Closed string mode $\epsilon_{\mu\nu}\alpha_{-1}^\mu\tilde{\alpha}_{-1}^\nu|0\rangle$ is massless ($m^2=0$ from level-matching), spin-2 (left+right movers), symmetric tensor = **graviton**. Coupling to metric from worldsheet $\int \sqrt{-h}h^{ab}g_{\mu\nu}\partial_a X^\mu\partial_b X^\nu$ reproduces GR with $G_N \sim g_s^2\ell_s^8$ in 10D. Gravity **emerges automatically**—string theory's strongest evidence. Not imposed, predicted!

**Problem 4: B** — Schwarzschild BH: Euclidean continuation near horizon requires periodic imaginary time $\beta = 8\pi GM$ → temperature $T_H = \hbar c^3/(8\pi GMk_B)$. Solar mass: $T_H \sim 10^{-7}$ K (tiny). Micro BH ($M \sim M_{\text{Pl}}$): $T_H \sim 10^{32}$ K! Entropy $S = A/(4\ell_{\text{Pl}}^2)$ (area law). String theory counts microstates (D-branes) matching exactly—major success. Holography: information on surface.

**Problem 5: B** — String compactifications on different Calabi-Yau manifolds, flux choices, branes give $\sim 10^{500}$ distinct 4D effective theories (vacua). Each has different particle content, couplings, cosmological constant. **No known principle** selects our vacuum—landscape loss of predictivity. Anthropic selection? ("We exist in one allowing observers.") Swampland program constraints? Eternal inflation populates all vacua? Unsolved problem undermining string theory's claim as unique "theory of everything."
