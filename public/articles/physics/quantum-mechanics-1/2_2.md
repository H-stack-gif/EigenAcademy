# Quantum Mechanics Topic 2.2: Harmonic Oscillator (Analytical and Algebraic Methods)

The quantum harmonic oscillator is the most important exactly solvable system in physics. It describes molecular vibrations, quantized electromagnetic fields (photons), phonons in solids, and provides the foundation for quantum field theory. The potential $V(x) = \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2x^2$ seems innocuous—just a parabola—yet solving the time-independent Schrödinger equation $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\psi'' + \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2x^2\psi = E\psi$ reveals profound structure.

Two approaches unlock the oscillator's secrets. The **analytical method** solves the differential equation via series expansions, yielding Hermite polynomials and energy levels $E_n = \hbar\omega(n + \frac{1}{2})$. The **algebraic method** (due to Dirac) introduces ladder operators $\hat{a}_\pm$ that raise or lower energy by $\hbar\omega$, bypassing differential equations entirely. This algebraic approach is so powerful that it generalizes to quantum field theory, where $\hat{a}_\pm$ create and annihilate particles.

This article presents both methods completely. We'll solve the differential equation analytically through Hermite polynomials, construct ladder operators from position and momentum, derive energy quantization from commutators alone, compute wavefunctions using ladder operations, and prove the ground state is a minimum uncertainty state satisfying $\Delta x \Delta p = \hbar/2$. You'll see two radically different paths to the same quantum truth.

## Analytical Solution: Differential Equation Method

**Schrödinger equation**:
$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2\psi}{dx^2} + \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2x^2\psi = E\psi$$

Introduce dimensionless variable: $\xi = \sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{\hbar}}x$

### Example 1: Dimensionless Form

Rewrite equation in terms of $\xi$.

**Solution:**

$$\frac{d}{dx} = \sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{\hbar}}\frac{d}{d\xi}$$

$$\frac{d^2}{dx^2} = \frac{m\omega}{\hbar}\frac{d^2}{d\xi^2}$$

Substitute:
$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \cdot \frac{m\omega}{\hbar}\frac{d^2\psi}{d\xi^2} + \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 \cdot \frac{\hbar}{m\omega}\xi^2\psi = E\psi$$

$$-\frac{\hbar\omega}{2}\frac{d^2\psi}{d\xi^2} + \frac{\hbar\omega}{2}\xi^2\psi = E\psi$$

Define $\epsilon = 2E/(\hbar\omega)$:

$$\frac{d^2\psi}{d\xi^2} = (\xi^2 - \epsilon)\psi$$

**Answer:** Dimensionless eigenvalue equation (cleaner form)

### Example 2: Asymptotic Behavior

For large $|\xi| \to \infty$, what is $\psi(\xi)$?

**Solution:**

For $\xi^2 \gg \epsilon$:
$$\frac{d^2\psi}{d\xi^2} \approx \xi^2\psi$$

Try $\psi \sim e^{-\alpha\xi^2}$:
$$\psi' = -2\alpha\xi e^{-\alpha\xi^2}$$
$$\psi'' = (-2\alpha + 4\alpha^2\xi^2)e^{-\alpha\xi^2}$$

For large $\xi$: $\psi'' \approx 4\alpha^2\xi^2\psi$

Compare to $\psi'' = \xi^2\psi$: need $4\alpha^2 = 1 \Rightarrow \alpha = \pm 1/2$

Choose $\alpha = +1/2$ (normalizable, $\psi \to 0$ as $|\xi| \to \infty$).

**Answer:** Asymptotic form $\psi(\xi) \sim e^{-\xi^2/2}$ (Gaussian envelope)

### Example 3: Hermite Polynomial Solutions

Factor out Gaussian: $\psi(\xi) = h(\xi)e^{-\xi^2/2}$

Find equation for $h(\xi)$.

**Solution:**

$$\psi' = h'e^{-\xi^2/2} - h\xi e^{-\xi^2/2}$$
$$\psi'' = h''e^{-\xi^2/2} - 2h'\xi e^{-\xi^2/2} + h(\xi^2 - 1)e^{-\xi^2/2}$$

Substitute into $\psi'' = (\xi^2 - \epsilon)\psi$:

$$h''e^{-\xi^2/2} - 2h'\xi e^{-\xi^2/2} + h(\xi^2 - 1)e^{-\xi^2/2} = (\xi^2 - \epsilon)he^{-\xi^2/2}$$

Cancel $e^{-\xi^2/2}$ and $\xi^2$ terms:

$$h'' - 2\xi h' + (\epsilon - 1)h = 0$$

This is **Hermite's differential equation**!

**Answer:** $h(\xi)$ are Hermite polynomials $H_n(\xi)$ with $\epsilon = 2n + 1$

### Example 4: Energy Quantization from Series

From $\epsilon = 2n + 1$ and $\epsilon = 2E/(\hbar\omega)$, find energy.

**Solution:**

$$\frac{2E_n}{\hbar\omega} = 2n + 1$$

$$E_n = \hbar\omega\left(n + \frac{1}{2}\right)$$ for $n = 0, 1, 2, 3, \ldots$

**Key features**:
- Equally spaced: $E_{n+1} - E_n = \hbar\omega$ (constant)
- Zero-point energy: $E_0 = \frac{1}{2}\hbar\omega > 0$
- Linear in $n$ (unlike infinite well's $n^2$)

**Answer:** $E_n = \hbar\omega(n + \frac{1}{2})$ (equally spaced ladder)

### Example 5: Ground State Wavefunction

For $n = 0$, $H_0(\xi) = 1$ (constant). Find $\psi_0(\xi)$.

**Solution:**

$$\psi_0(\xi) = A_0 H_0(\xi)e^{-\xi^2/2} = A_0 e^{-\xi^2/2}$$

Normalize:
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}|A_0|^2 e^{-\xi^2} d\xi = 1$$

Gaussian integral: $\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-\xi^2} d\xi = \sqrt{\pi}$

$$|A_0|^2\sqrt{\pi} = 1 \Rightarrow A_0 = \pi^{-1/4}$$

In terms of $x$:
$$\psi_0(x) = \left(\frac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{1/4}e^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}$$

**Answer:** Gaussian ground state (no nodes, minimum uncertainty)

## Algebraic Solution: Ladder Operator Method

Define **ladder operators**:

$$\hat{a}_+ = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}}(m\omega\hat{x} - i\hat{p})$$ (raising/creation)

$$\hat{a}_- = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}}(m\omega\hat{x} + i\hat{p})$$ (lowering/annihilation)

### Example 6: Commutation Relation

Compute $[\hat{a}_-, \hat{a}_+]$.

**Solution:**

$$[\hat{a}_-, \hat{a}_+] = \frac{1}{2m\hbar\omega}[(m\omega\hat{x} + i\hat{p}), (m\omega\hat{x} - i\hat{p})]$$

Expand:
$$= \frac{1}{2m\hbar\omega}[m^2\omega^2[\hat{x}, \hat{x}] - im\omega[\hat{x}, \hat{p}] + im\omega[\hat{p}, \hat{x}] - i^2[\hat{p}, \hat{p}]]$$

Using $[\hat{x}, \hat{x}] = [\hat{p}, \hat{p}] = 0$ and $[\hat{x}, \hat{p}] = i\hbar$:

$$= \frac{1}{2m\hbar\omega}[-im\omega \cdot i\hbar + im\omega \cdot (-i\hbar)]$$

$$= \frac{1}{2m\hbar\omega}[m\omega\hbar + m\omega\hbar] = 1$$

**Answer:** $[\hat{a}_-, \hat{a}_+] = 1$ (canonical commutator for bosons)

### Example 7: Hamiltonian in Terms of Ladder Operators

Express $\hat{H} = \frac{\hat{p}^2}{2m} + \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2\hat{x}^2$ using $\hat{a}_\pm$.

**Solution:**

From definitions:
$$\hat{x} = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}(\hat{a}_+ + \hat{a}_-)$$
$$\hat{p} = i\sqrt{\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}}(\hat{a}_+ - \hat{a}_-)$$

Compute $\hat{p}^2$:
$$\hat{p}^2 = -\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}(\hat{a}_+ - \hat{a}_-)^2 = -\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}(\hat{a}_+^2 - \hat{a}_+\hat{a}_- - \hat{a}_-\hat{a}_+ + \hat{a}_-^2)$$

And $m^2\omega^2\hat{x}^2$:
$$m^2\omega^2\hat{x}^2 = \frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}(\hat{a}_+ + \hat{a}_-)^2 = \frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}(\hat{a}_+^2 + \hat{a}_+\hat{a}_- + \hat{a}_-\hat{a}_+ + \hat{a}_-^2)$$

Add for Hamiltonian:
$$\hat{H} = \frac{\hbar\omega}{2}[\hat{a}_+\hat{a}_- + \hat{a}_-\hat{a}_+]$$

Using $[\hat{a}_-, \hat{a}_+] = 1 \Rightarrow \hat{a}_-\hat{a}_+ = \hat{a}_+\hat{a}_- + 1$:

$$\hat{H} = \hbar\omega\left(\hat{a}_+\hat{a}_- + \frac{1}{2}\right)$$

**Answer:** $\hat{H} = \hbar\omega(\hat{N} + \frac{1}{2})$ where $\hat{N} = \hat{a}_+\hat{a}_-$ (number operator)

### Example 8: Ladder Operations

Show $\hat{H}\hat{a}_\pm|n\rangle = (E_n \pm \hbar\omega)\hat{a}_\pm|n\rangle$.

**Solution:**

Use $[\hat{H}, \hat{a}_+] = \hbar\omega\hat{a}_+$:

$$\hat{H}\hat{a}_+|n\rangle = \hat{a}_+\hat{H}|n\rangle + \hbar\omega\hat{a}_+|n\rangle$$

$$= \hat{a}_+(E_n|n\rangle) + \hbar\omega\hat{a}_+|n\rangle = (E_n + \hbar\omega)\hat{a}_+|n\rangle$$

So $\hat{a}_+|n\rangle$ is eigenstate with energy $E_n + \hbar\omega$!

Similarly, $[\hat{H}, \hat{a}_-] = -\hbar\omega\hat{a}_-$ gives:

$$\hat{H}\hat{a}_-|n\rangle = (E_n - \hbar\omega)\hat{a}_-|n\rangle$$

**Answer:** $\hat{a}_+$ raises energy by $\hbar\omega$, $\hat{a}_-$ lowers by $\hbar\omega$

### Example 9: Ground State from Annihilation

Require $\hat{a}_-|0\rangle = 0$ (cannot go below ground state).

Solve for $\psi_0(x)$.

**Solution:**

$$\hat{a}_-|0\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2m\hbar\omega}}(m\omega\hat{x} + i\hat{p})|0\rangle = 0$$

In position representation:
$$\left(m\omega x + i\left(-i\hbar\frac{d}{dx}\right)\right)\psi_0(x) = 0$$

$$m\omega x\psi_0 + \hbar\frac{d\psi_0}{dx} = 0$$

Separable ODE:
$$\frac{d\psi_0}{\psi_0} = -\frac{m\omega}{\hbar}x dx$$

$$\ln\psi_0 = -\frac{m\omega x^2}{2\hbar} + C$$

$$\psi_0(x) = Ae^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}$$

**Answer:** Same Gaussian as analytical method! ✓

### Example 10: Normalization Constants

If $\hat{a}_+|n\rangle = c_{n+1}|n+1\rangle$, find $c_{n+1}$.

**Solution:**

$\hat{a}_+|n\rangle = c_n|n+1\rangle$ (proportional to $|n+1\rangle$, need constant)

$$\langle n|\hat{a}_-\hat{a}_+|n\rangle = |c_n|^2$$

$$\langle n|\hat{a}_+\hat{a}_- + 1|n\rangle = n + 1$$

So $|c_n|^2 = n + 1 \Rightarrow c_n = \sqrt{n+1}$

**Answer:** $\hat{a}_+|n\rangle = \sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle$, $\hat{a}_-|n\rangle = \sqrt{n}|n-1\rangle$

### Example 11: Building Excited States

Express $|n\rangle$ in terms of $|0\rangle$.

**Solution:**

$$|1\rangle = \frac{\hat{a}_+}{\sqrt{1}}|0\rangle$$

$$|2\rangle = \frac{\hat{a}_+}{\sqrt{2}}|1\rangle = \frac{\hat{a}_+^2}{\sqrt{2 \cdot 1}}|0\rangle$$

$$|n\rangle = \frac{(\hat{a}_+)^n}{\sqrt{n!}}|0\rangle$$

**Answer:** Apply raising operator $n$ times to ground state (normalized)

## Minimum Uncertainty State

### Example 12: Computing $\Delta x$ and $\Delta p$ for Ground State

Show ground state saturates uncertainty: $\Delta x \Delta p = \hbar/2$.

**Solution:**

For $\psi_0(x) = \left(\frac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar}\right)^{1/4}e^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}$:

By symmetry: $\langle x \rangle = \langle p \rangle = 0$

$$\Delta x = \sqrt{\langle x^2 \rangle} = \sqrt{\int x^2|\psi_0|^2 dx}$$

Gaussian integral: $\langle x^2 \rangle = \frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}$

$$\Delta x = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}}$$

From $\langle p^2 \rangle = \langle -\hbar^2\psi_0''/\psi_0 \rangle$:

$$\langle p^2 \rangle = \frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}$$

$$\Delta p = \sqrt{\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}}$$

$$\Delta x \Delta p = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2m\omega}} \cdot \sqrt{\frac{m\hbar\omega}{2}} = \frac{\hbar}{2}$$ ✓

**Answer:** Ground state is minimum uncertainty state (equality in Heisenberg relation)

### Example 13: Why Oscillator is Universal

Any smooth potential near minimum looks like harmonic oscillator. Why?

**Solution:**

Taylor expand $V(x)$ near equilibrium $x_0$ where $V'(x_0) = 0$:

$$V(x) = V(x_0) + 0 \cdot (x - x_0) + \frac{1}{2}V''(x_0)(x - x_0)^2 + O((x-x_0)^3)$$

For small oscillations around $x_0$: keep only quadratic term.

Define $k = V''(x_0) > 0$ (stable equilibrium):

$$V(x) \approx V(x_0) + \frac{1}{2}k(x - x_0)^2$$

This is harmonic oscillator with $\omega = \sqrt{k/m}$!

**Applications**:
- Molecular vibrations (bonds as springs)
- Crystal lattice phonons
- Electromagnetic cavity modes

**Answer:** Harmonic oscillator approximates any potential near minimum (universality)

## Answer Key

**Problem 1: B**
$E_n = \hbar\omega(n + \frac{1}{2})$ for $n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$
Equally spaced ladder (unique to harmonic oscillator)
Zero-point energy $E_0 = \frac{\hbar\omega}{2}$
Energy spacing $\Delta E = \hbar\omega$ (constant)
Not quadratic like infinite well

**Problem 2: B**
$\hat{a}_+|n\rangle = \sqrt{n+1}|n+1\rangle$ (raises $n$ by 1)
Increases energy: $E_{n+1} = E_n + \hbar\omega$
Creation operator (adds quantum)
$\hat{a}_-$ lowers energy (annihilation)
Foundation for quantum field theory

**Problem 3: C**
$E_0 = \frac{1}{2}\hbar\omega$ (zero-point energy)
Cannot be zero (uncertainty principle)
Confinement by potential requires kinetic energy
Minimum allowed by $\Delta x \Delta p \geq \hbar/2$
Even at absolute zero, oscillator has energy

**Problem 4: B**
$[\hat{a}_-, \hat{a}_+] = 1$ (number, not operator)
Canonical commutation for bosonic ladder operators
Derived from $[\hat{x}, \hat{p}] = i\hbar$
Implies $\hat{a}_-\hat{a}_+ = \hat{a}_+\hat{a}_- + 1$
Used to express Hamiltonian

**Problem 5: C**
$\psi_0(x) \propto e^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}$ (Gaussian)
No nodes (ground state never has nodes)
Minimum uncertainty: $\Delta x \Delta p = \hbar/2$
Same from both analytical and algebraic methods
Most "classical" quantum state
