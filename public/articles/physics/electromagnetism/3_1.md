# Electromagnetism — Advanced Topics (1.3)

Placeholder for advanced electromagnetism topics: radiation, boundary value problems, waveguides, and introduction to electrodynamics in media.

## Practice Problems

1. Describe qualitatively what determines radiation pattern for an oscillating dipole.
2. What boundary conditions must E and B satisfy at the interface of two media?

## Answer Key

1. Dipole radiation dominated by time-varying dipole moment and its derivatives; pattern depends on oscillation frequency and orientation.
2. Tangential components of E and H are continuous across boundaries (with surface currents/charges producing jumps); normal components of D and B change according to surface charge/monopole terms.
