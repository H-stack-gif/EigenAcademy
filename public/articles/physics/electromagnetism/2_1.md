# Electrostatics — Topic 2.1

Basic electrostatics: Coulomb's law, electric field of point charges, superposition, potential, and simple boundary conditions.

## Overview

- Coulomb's law and field from a point charge
- Superposition principle
- Relationship between electric potential and electric field

## Practice Problems

1. Determine the electric field at a point on the axis of a uniformly charged rod (assume finite length).
2. Compute the potential due to a dipole at distances much larger than the dipole separation.

## Answer Key

1. Sketch: integrate contributions from each charge element and simplify; show limiting cases.
2. Answer: potential ~ p·r / (4πε0 r^3) where p is the dipole moment — leading order term in multipole expansion.
