# Magnetostatics — Topic 2.2

Magnetostatics primer: Biot–Savart law, magnetic fields of steady currents, Ampère's circuital law in magnetostatic limit, and magnetic dipole moment.

## Overview

- Biot–Savart law
- Curl and divergence properties of B in steady currents
- Magnetic dipole and multipole ideas

## Practice Problems

1. Use Biot–Savart law to find the magnetic field on the axis of a circular current loop.
2. Show that for steady currents ∇·B = 0 and discuss physical meaning.

## Answer Key

1. Sketch: integral reduces to result proportional to μ0 I R^2 / (2 (R^2 + z^2)^{3/2}) along axis.
2. Answer: ∇·B = 0 follows because there are no magnetic monopoles; field lines are continuous loops.
