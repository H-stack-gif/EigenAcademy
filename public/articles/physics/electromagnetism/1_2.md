# Electromagnetic Waves — Topic 1.2

Intro to electromagnetic waves, propagation through vacuum and media, wave equations derived from Maxwell's equations, plane waves and polarization.

## Overview

- The wave equation for E and B
- Speed of light and medium parameters (ε, μ)
- Plane waves, polarization, and boundary behavior

## Practice Problems

1. Starting from Maxwell's equations in free space, derive the wave equation for the electric field.
2. A plane wave E = E0 cos(k·r - ωt) is traveling in the z-direction — what is the corresponding B-field and its direction?

## Answer Key

1. Sketch of derivation: take curl of Faraday's law and substitute Ampère–Maxwell, use vector identities to get ∇^2E - μ0ε0∂^2E/∂t^2 = 0.
2. For a wave traveling in z, B is orthogonal to E and k; if E is in x, B will be in y with amplitude E0/c.
